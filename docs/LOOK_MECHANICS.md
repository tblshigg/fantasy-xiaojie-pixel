# Pixel human gaze mechanics

Feet, lower torso, short trousers and ivory sneakers stay planted at the same camera angle. The oversized red hood frames the same black-fringe human face in every pose. The neck and head turn naturally within the hood; the hood opening follows slightly without moving the entire body.

Large original eyes, eyelids, nose and face surface lead gaze. Keep the same face, highlights and eye design. Horizontal gaze uses a modest head yaw so the nose and both pupils shift toward the indicated screen edge. Vertical gaze uses obvious head pitch: chin lifts and eyes look above the fringe for up; chin tucks and eyes aim below the face center for down. Maintain a visible face in every direction.

Cardinals in screen coordinates: up, screen-right, down, screen-left. Diagonals combine visible yaw and pitch. Use smooth intermediate steps; no whole-body turntable, sprite rotation, camera change, replacement eyes, or pupil-only direction. The character remains recognizably the same cute front-standing red-hood boy.
